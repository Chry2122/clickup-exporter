Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This policy is provided by Chry's Tools.

This policy explains what information is involved when you use the Attachment Exporter for ClickUp website and Chrome extension (the “Extension”). We designed the product so that we do not need to collect your ClickUp data on a server of our own.

1. Who we are

Chry's Tools makes and offers the Extension. For questions about this policy, email licatagiuseppechristian@gmail.com.

2. This website

This website is a static site. We do not run an application server that logs you in, create an account for you, or set our own tracking cookies. If the site is hosted on GitHub Pages or a similar host, that provider may process standard technical data such as IP addresses in accordance with its own policies. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

3. Data the Extension keeps on your device

The Extension stores the following locally in chrome.storage.local on the device where it is installed:

  • Your ClickUp personal API token (a token that typically starts with pk_), if you choose to save it.
  • License-related state for Pro, such as a license key and activation status, if you upgrade.

This information stays on your computer unless you remove the Extension or clear its storage. We do not operate a backend that receives your token or your ClickUp attachments.

4. Network requests the Extension makes

The Extension does not send traffic to a Chry's Tools server. It may contact:

  • ClickUp at api.clickup.com, using the official API and the token you provide, to list workspaces, spaces, folders, and lists, and to download task attachments you choose to export. ClickUp processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Dodo Payments at live.dodopayments.com, if you purchase or activate a Pro license, to activate and validate a license key. Dodo Payments is the merchant of record for paid licenses and processes payment and license data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Attachments you export are downloaded to your device and packed into a ZIP file there. We do not host, store, or archive those files.

5. Payments

If you buy Pro, checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, which acts as merchant of record and may collect your name, email address, payment details, billing address, and tax information (including EU VAT where applicable). Chry's Tools does not collect or store your card numbers.

6. Chrome Web Store

When the Extension is listed on the Chrome Web Store, Google may process installation and usage data according to Google’s policies. That listing is not live yet.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If European data-protection law applies, we process any personal data we handle on these bases: providing the website and Extension at your request (contract or pre-contract steps), compliance with legal obligations, and, where needed, our legitimate interest in answering support requests and improving a product you already use. Payment processing is carried out by Dodo Payments as merchant of record.

8. Retention

We do not keep a customer database of ClickUp tokens or exported files. Data on your device remains until you delete it. Support emails you send to licatagiuseppechristian@gmail.com are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any legal record-keeping.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal data, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). To exercise rights that we can actually fulfill, email licatagiuseppechristian@gmail.com. Requests about payment records may need to be directed to Dodo Payments as well, since they process the purchase.

10. Children

The Extension is intended for people who already have a ClickUp account and the authority to use an API token. It is not directed at children under 16.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the product or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site or Extension after an update means you should read the new version.
